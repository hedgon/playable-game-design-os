## Fact-check corrections (2026-10-05)
Applied from rhythm-heaven.factcheck.md. Sources are the ones the fact-check read; Metacritic, OpenCritic and Steam were not loadable.
- Eurogamer: one writer called Tengoku the best GBA game of 2006; the staff ranked it 36th. Source: Wikipedia, Rhythm Tengoku, Reception. Changed in complaints and reception[0].
- Motion: now "tiring and less accurate than buttons". "Strenuous" is in Iwata Asks Fever p1; "more accurate" buttons is Wikipedia, Rhythm Heaven Fever, Development. "Imprecise" removed (not in the interview).
- Song/setting pairings: rewritten as surprise, not planned contrast (artist drew what each song suggested; producer did not overrule). Iwata Asks Fever p2. Art mechanism and world evidence changed to match.
- Team size: "about three times as much, comparatively speaking" is Iwata's wording (Iwata Asks Fever p4), not "staff tripled per developers".
- Remix tests: removed the claim that remix tests were too hard. The too-hard tests were general playtests; examples and demos were added to broaden appeal (Iwata Asks Fever p4-p6). Teach and replay changed; replay now cites the "fewer notes or slower tempo" remark (p1).
- Groove charts: number one for its first three weeks and again the week to 13 Sep 2026 (seventh time). Famitsu, 17 Sep 2026; Wikipedia, Groove.
- Groove latency: docked TV play criticised by Nintendo Life and Shacknews (worse after calibration); Siliconera on local multiplayer. Wikipedia, Groove, Reception. Added to complaints and reception[4]; gauge (timing feedback) and text-to-speech (visually impaired players) now separated.
- Cancer: Groove half cut; vocal cord removal was during Megamix development (Wikipedia, Megamix).
- Bits & Bops: 16 minigames plus 4 mixtapes (Siliconera review); $15.99 and "around $16 on Steam" removed because the store page could not be checked. Siliconera's "trust your ears, one minigame felt that way" added to lineage and misses.
- Melatonin "calm, coherent setting" comparison removed (NWR does not say it); replaced with marked judgement. Melatonin stage count 21 (NWR).
- Business claim narrowed to 2008-2016 lag, then Groove's same-day worldwide launch (store page, Wikipedia). Re-recording cause marked judgement. Heaven Studio reading marked judgement.
- ui.cost mismatched-scene claim marked judgement. "RPG hybrid" removed from lore compare. "in my judgement" changed to "Judgement:".
- receptionLesson: Fever praise marked judgement; stylus point reframed as a development fact.
- Images: e1 URL corrected to LaunchBox 20455; e2, e3 moved to LaunchBox 12041, 13541 with licence capture; e4 credit is a LaunchBox placeholder (Megamix id not found); alts no longer claim stylus, two buttons, two screens or a flat style.
