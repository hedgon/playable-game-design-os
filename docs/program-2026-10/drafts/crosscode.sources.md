Read, secondary.
- Steam: release 20 Sep 2018; 7 areas, 7 dungeons, 120+ enemy types, 30+ bosses, 90+ combat arts, 100+ quests, 60+ tracks, 30-80 hours; 93% positive of 10,262 English reviews (at fetch, 2026-10-05); header URL. https://store.steampowered.com/app/368340/CrossCode/ . Read, primary.
