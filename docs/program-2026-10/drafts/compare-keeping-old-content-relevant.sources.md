- Nerfs: a window of about two weeks to disenchant for full cost, not an automatic refund; Hall of Fame moves do refund. https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Arcane_dust.
- Hall of Fame: put in past tense ("From 2017"), since no source shows moves after the 2021 Core set; diagram cell now "Hall of Fame moves, nerf windows".
- Slay the Spire: run-comparison sentence reworded; telemetry-led balancing added (pick rates, win rates in early access) from library slay-the-spire; source swapped to https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-i-slay-the-spire-i-s-devs-use-data-to-balance-their-roguelike-deck-builder (replaces the GDC preview page).
