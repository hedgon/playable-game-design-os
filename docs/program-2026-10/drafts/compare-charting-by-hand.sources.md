## Judgement
- Effects are timed to the music in the same editor: library art lens reasoning; the editor and its effects are on Steam.
- Effects never change what counts as on time: inference, no developer says it.
- Checking by playing with the picture hidden: our suggestion, not a source claim.
- Expert charts feel like reading arrows; big-swing scoring tires arms: library complaints, reviewer-based, not re-read.
- Every effect is spent on difficulty, so less on clarity: our reading.
