## Judgement
- Item as the link, boss as the last use: our reading of the library loop.
- Later CrossCode dungeons add steps, not verbs: the library marks this as the editor's reading; no source places fatigue there.
- "Often weak to the dungeon item": no per-boss claim is made because the Boss Keys episodes could not be read.
- "Cheap to read, expensive to repeat once rooms reset": reasoning from the reset cost.
- Zelda puzzle and fight stay separate: our reading, matching the library's compare line.
