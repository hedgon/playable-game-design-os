## Revision after the independent fact-check (2026-10-05)

- Metroid Dread Icon Highlight (select an icon, press Y; highlights similar points of interest across the map; can show all doors a newly gained ability opens; areas with hidden items glow). Source: Nintendo, Metroid Dread Report Vol. 9, https://metroid.nintendo.com/dread/news/metroid-dread-report-vol-9 . Read by the fact-checker (primary). Replaces the earlier "more detail than earlier games" wording.
- The Dread squeeze-gap example was removed (unsupported). The replacement traversal example is Super Metroid's Speed Booster opening routes through already-crossed rooms: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Metroid (read, secondary).
- Segmenting the world and points of no return: Mark Brown, Game Maker's Toolkit, "Why You Didn't Get Lost in Metroid Dread", video page https://amara.org/en-gb/videos/Aci4iDbgsAtY . Confirmed by the fact-checker through the video description and summaries; the transcript itself still returns 403.
- Hollow Knight prices: community wiki only (no Team Cherry source exists). Corrected: most pins 100 Geo (Whispering Root Pin 150, Warrior's Grave Pin 180; markers 100 to 210), Iselda also sells maps at a higher price (Cornifer charges 75%), Cornifer has no map for every area. https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Cornifer , https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Iselda . The player places bought pins and markers; they do not draw.
- Zelda: dungeon item opens the dungeon's last door and often an overworld obstacle (game id `zelda`, library entry in src/18-games-series.js; the fact-checker cites it). Majora's Mask Bombers' Notebook as a memory aid: same entry. Mark Brown's Boss Keys series as practitioner analysis: https://amara.org/v/C3BF1 (transcript not read; summary only).
- Mega Man X: Storm Eagle's stage shorts Spark Mandrill's traps; Chill Penguin freezes the lava in Flame Mammoth's stage. https://vgmuseum.com/mrp/mm-stages3.htm , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mega_Man_X_(video_game) . Secondary; the game itself is the primary.
- Dark Souls warp rule: Lordvessel given after Ornstein and Smough in Anor Londo; warp from most bonfires, only to a small set of lit ones. https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Lordvessel (fan wiki; in-game item text is the primary; not read by me). The Undead Parish to Firelink Shrine lift as the named elevator shortcut is from the fact-checker's note (canonical example, not separately sourced).
- Elden Ring: travel from the map to any discovered site of grace, not inside dungeons or in combat. https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Mechanics (surfaced by search, wiki; read as a search summary only). The Wikipedia page's wording was more general and does not contradict it.
- Skyrim: free fast travel from any exterior location to any discovered location; carriages are the paid alternative. https://content1.m.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Carriage (via the fact-checker, community wiki; Bethesda manual not found).
- Unity: Time.time is scaled by Time.timeScale, and Time.realtimeSinceStartupAsDouble is preferred for long sessions. https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Time-time.html , https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/Time-realtimeSinceStartup.html . Godot Time.get_ticks_msec(): https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_time.html .
- Heatmaps: the topic's advice to read a hot corridor against the doors open to the player is my own reasoning, not a sourced claim.
