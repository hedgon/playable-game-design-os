- CrossCode (Radical Fish Games): one ball-launching mechanic for combat and puzzles; puzzle elements switches, boxes, ice pellets, barriers, water bubbles, fans; elemental switching; development began 2011; Indiegogo Feb 2015 (target EUR 80,000); PC release 20 Sep 2018. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CrossCode . - Felix Klein (Radical Fish Games) on the origin: Yoshi's Island made top-down and turned into an RPG; battles with puzzle-heavy mechanics and puzzles that are fast paced; dungeons modelled on Zelda. https://www.siliconera.com/crosscode-developer-reflects-rpgs-six-years-evolution/ . Read, press interview with developer. The "Yoshi's Island" paraphrase is from here; the exact wording is not verified.
